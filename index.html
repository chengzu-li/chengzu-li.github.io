About me

I am a final-year PhD student in Computation, Cognition and Language at Language Technology Lab in the University of Cambridge, supervised by Dr. Ivan Vulić, Prof. Anna Korhonen and Prof. Serge Belongie. My research is supported by Cambridge Trust. I am a member of Jesus College.

I am currently interning at Google and previously interned as a Research Intern at Microsoft Research.

Before starting my PhD, I received my MPhil degree in Advanced Computer Science at the Department of Computer Science, supervised by Prof. Simone Teufel, supported by Cambridge Trust. I was an undergraduate student in Automation at Xi'an Jiaotong University.

I am looking for full-time Research Scientist roles; feel free to contact me regarding research collaborations and potential job opportunities.

Research

My research interests center around Visual Intelligence with a specific focus on spatial reasoning [New Recipes], covering Multimodal LLMs, Video Generation Model and Multimodal Agents.

Specifically, my work focuses on:

  • Visual Intelligence and Multimodal Reasoning: [Visual Planning, ICLR Oral] [MVoT, ICML]

  • Multimodal Agentic Reasoning: [UnifiedSKG, EMNLP Oral] [BINDER, ICLR Spotlight]

  • World Models & Video Generation for Planning: [Thinking in Frames]

  • Evaluation and Capability Understanding: [TopViewRS, EMNLP Oral] [11Plus] [VIBE]

I'm also interested in the potential downstream application scenarios of multimodal reasoning.
